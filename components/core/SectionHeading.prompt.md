Overline + display title + lede — the opener for every section.

```jsx
<SectionHeading overline="Since 2015" title="Eleven years on Sierra Lakes" lede="Color, cuts and treatments by stylists who have worked the same chairs for a decade." />
```

Titles are always uppercase Bodoni. Set `onLight` on bone surfaces. `align="center"` caps the lede at 680px.
