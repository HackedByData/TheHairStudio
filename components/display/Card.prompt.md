Surface container — square, hairline-bordered.

```jsx
<Card tone="dark" interactive onClick={open}>…</Card>
<Card tone="light" soft padding={40}>…</Card>
```

`dark` is the default (#141414). `void` is pure black for full-bleed sections. `light` is the bone card from the printed flier — pair it with `soft` for the 14px radius. Hover lifts 2px and warms the border to oxblood.
