A stylist highlight — portrait, name, specialty, handle.

```jsx
<StylistCard name="Angie" role="Owner · Master stylist" specialties={['Balayage', 'Color correction', 'Extensions']} handle="@_thehairstudio_" years="12 years" />
```

Grid of three or four. `role` is oxblood uppercase; specialties are joined with middots.
